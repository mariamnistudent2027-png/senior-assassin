Official Rules

  • Safety & Protection: You must wear swim goggles to be safe. You cannot target or tag anyone while you are wearing them.
  • Permitted Times & Zones: Play is allowed ONLY during PE class, Break time, or outside of school. Play is strictly banned in classrooms, hallways, restrooms, and during the Morning Line.
  • Instant Proof: If targeted while wearing goggles, you must show them to your attacker at that exact moment, or you will be eliminated.
  • Round System: The game is split into rounds lasting exactly 1 week.
  • Absence Rule: Being absent from school for 2 consecutive weeks results in permanent disqualification.

Points System

  • Tagging your assigned target: +3 points
  • Getting targeted and hit: -2 points
  • Your target is absent the entire round: +2 points
  • Getting hit while your own target is absent: -1 point
